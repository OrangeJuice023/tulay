# Stories and Our Story proposal

Status: proposal for review. No code changes in this phase.

## Our Story — `/our-story` (P1)

A brand and mission page. Allowed sources only (no invented history):

- **Problem:** career moves are solo missions; asking for help feels hard.
- **Why Tulay exists:** hackathon-born MVP (Meta Global AI Developer
  Hackathon, Oct–Nov 2026) testing whether AI can help people help each other.
- **Philosophy:** relationships are the goal; grounded recommendations;
  reciprocity without pressure; consent before outreach; no unproven claims
  (see `docs/PROJECT_DIRECTION.md`).
- **The bridge metaphor:** "Tulay" is presented as "bridge" (existing brand
  copy) — the product is the span, members do the crossing, together.
- **What we don't claim:** no founder biographies (none on record), no
  outcomes data, no guarantees.

## Community Stories — `/stories` (P1)

Four-beat journeys rendered **from recorded collaboration data**: the
challenge (need description) → the connection (who matched, why) → the
collaborative action (next steps) → the recorded outcome (helped? + note).

- Demo stories are **generated from synthetic members** and labeled
  "Illustrative demo story — fictional members" on every card. Never
  presented as testimonials.
- Real stories (post-launch, opt-in only) reuse the same template with the
  label removed and consent recorded.
- Empty state: Arko-empty + "Stories begin with a first collaboration" linking
  to `/start`. This makes Stories a growth surface for the engine, not a feed.

Both pages share the landing's flat illustration language and Arko (idle on
Our Story, celebration on completed-story cards).

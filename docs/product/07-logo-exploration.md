# Logo exploration: three original directions

Status: design criteria + directions for review. **The current mark stays.**
No files created, no replacement made. Arko remains a separate mascot, never
the logo.

## Current mark assessment (from `apps/web/components/Logo.tsx`)

Two figures joined by a bridge where deck + pier read as a **T** and the arch
carries them; forest `currentColor` + one coral figure. Conceptually it
already says bridge + people, but the reading is abstract at small sizes: the
T is discovered, not seen, and "two humans" requires explanation.

## Design criteria (all three directions)

- Must read at 16px favicon and 32px header; one idea, no fine detail.
- Forest `#176B46` primary, coral `#FF7A59` single accent, works in
  monochrome (`currentColor` pattern as today).
- Clearspace = arch height; never recolor the coral element; never on busy
  photography.
- Pair with the existing Inter extrabold wordmark (type unchanged).

## Direction 1 — Minimal bridge arch connecting two endpoints

A single flat arch spanning two dots (the endpoints/people abstracted).
Strongest at tiny sizes; most ownable as an app icon. Risk: generic "bridge"
without Tulay's human warmth — needs the coral endpoint to carry meaning.

## Direction 2 — Bridge-shaped letter T

The deck forms the T's bar, a single pier the stem, arched slightly. Keeps
today's hidden-T idea but makes it the whole mark: name + bridge in one
gesture. Risk: reads as a plain T without the arch curve; needs the curve to
dominate.

## Direction 3 — Two human figures connected by a bridge motif

Closest to today: two rounded figures standing on / joined by an arch deck.
Clearest story ("people, connected"), weakest at 16px. Risk: mascot-adjacent
clutter — must stay geometric, never illustrative, to keep distance from Arko.

## Recommendation

Direction 1 for product surfaces (clarity at size) with Direction 3's story
told by Arko alongside it — logo stays abstract, mascot stays human. Decide
post-MVP; until then the current mark stands.
